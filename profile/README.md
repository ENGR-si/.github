ENGR is a web and mobile app company in Ljubljana, Slovenia. We design and build apps for clients, and we run two products of our own. Client work keeps us sharp; our own products keep us honest.

## Our products

### [anon.li](https://anon.li) · privacy toolkit

Built in-house. Email aliases keep your real address out of other people's databases, and files and form answers are end-to-end encrypted in your browser, so the server only ever stores ciphertext it can't read. Source code is available on [github.com/anondotli](https://github.com/anondotli).

### [pwn.guide](https://pwn.guide) · learn ethical hacking

Acquired in August 2026. More than two hundred hands-on tutorials turn real attacks and fresh CVEs into step-by-step lessons, and full courses prepare you for the CompTIA Security+ and PenTest+ exams. Everything is written for authorised testing and defence.

## Client work

Company websites, online stores, and web and mobile apps, taken from the first conversation through design, development and launch. The people who build our own products are the ones who build yours, and there's no handoff between separate design and development teams.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Talk** | You show us the business, the current site, and what isn't working. |
| **2. Plan** | We decide which pages are needed, what they say, and how they look. |
| **3. Make** | We design and build it. You review real pages as the work moves forward. |
| **4. Launch** | We test everything, connect the domain and analytics, and put it live. |

After launch we keep running what we ship: support, releases and maintenance.
